## Research & Publications

- **[Sampling Budget Biases Apparent Metastable-State Counts in Molecular Simulation](https://github.com/imjbassi/state-count-sampling-bias)** — Controlled synthetic landscapes and two independent alanine-dipeptide trajectories show how sampling budget can inflate apparent state counts. Submitted to JCTC on September 15, 2026; the [ChemRxiv preprint](https://chemrxiv.org/doc/3ca81cab-c111-4416-99f8-0b2654e38da7) is under review, with a [versioned Zenodo dataset](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22754433).

- **[Do Published HumanEval Rankings Survive Local Deployment? A Five-Model Reproduction on a Consumer GPU](https://github.com/imjbassi/local-deployment-of-transformer-based-code-assistants)** — A pinned five-model RTX 4070 reproduction found one resolved ranking reversal and Kendall’s tau-b of 0.8. Version 1.0 is a technical report, not a venue submission, arXiv paper, or DOI-bearing archive. **[PDF](https://github.com/imjbassi/local-deployment-of-transformer-based-code-assistants/blob/main/paper/output/pdf/Do_Published_HumanEval_Rankings_Survive_Local_Deployment.pdf)**

- **[When Does INT8 Actually Accelerate Host-CPU Inference? A Reproducible Audit of MobileNetV2 Conversion and Packaging](https://github.com/imjbassi/efficient-ai-edge-deployment)** — Shows that INT8 latency depends on backend delegation and quantization granularity, alongside paired accuracy and packaging controls. Released as [v1.2.0 on Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22758817), but not submitted for peer review.

- **[Conditioning and Directionality in Adversarial Transfer on CIFAR-10](https://github.com/imjbassi/transferability-of-adversarial-attacks)** — Denominator-aware full-test study across three architectures and three training seeds. Submitted to Transactions on Machine Learning Research and [under review on OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=0wQCiGLqZx), with an [archived reproducibility release](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22737839).

- **[Complete Patient-Level Leakage Among Traceable Images in a Widely Used Brain Tumor MRI Benchmark](https://github.com/imjbassi/brain-tumor-mri-classification)** — Finds complete patient overlap among traceable tumor test images plus substantial duplicate contamination, and releases patient-disjoint folds. Available as a preprint and [Zenodo artifact](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22755070); not yet submitted to a journal.

- **[Not All Bad Demonstrations Are Equally Bad: Quantifying How Demonstration Failure Modes Degrade Closed-Loop Policy Performance](https://github.com/imjbassi/demo-quality-robustness)** — Controlled study of 660 policy fits across five failure modes, two policy families, ten seeds, and transition-matched controls. Submitted to the CoRL 2026 Learning from Corrections and Interventions workshop on [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=DBSdaBnibW).

- **[Smoothness Ranks Skill, Not Success: An Audit of Published Trajectory-Smoothness Curation Metrics Under Episode-Length Controls](https://github.com/imjbassi/smoothness-audit)** — Base preprint plus three workshop submissions under review: [CoRL WEBP](https://openreview.net/forum?id=8HHUxS9GcK), [NeurIPS RoboPAD](https://openreview.net/forum?id=wj9Kj3hsJs), and [CoRL Oops, I Erred](https://openreview.net/forum?id=7C6WROjCKQ).
